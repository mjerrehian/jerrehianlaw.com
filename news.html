Superior Court Vacates Conviction Under Philadelphia-Only Gun Law

Originally posted June 23, 2025. Revised November 2026.

On June 23, 2025, the Pennsylvania Superior Court vacated the judgment of sentence of a defendant convicted under 18 Pa.C.S. § 6108 for openly carrying a handgun on a Philadelphia street without a license. The Court held that § 6108 violated the right to equal protection, as applied. Commonwealth v. Sumpter, 340 A.3d 977 (Pa. Super. 2025).

Mr. Jerrehian represented the defendant in Philadelphia Municipal Court as an Assistant Defender at the Defender Association of Philadelphia. There, he filed the original motion to dismiss, raising the constitutional challenge to § 6108. Jerrehian Law did not handle the appeal.

Everywhere else in Pennsylvania, adults 18 and older may openly carry a firearm without a license. Section 6108 makes the same conduct a crime in Philadelphia alone, unfairly targeting the people of Philadelphia.

The Superior Court found that unequal treatment unconstitutional. Because the right to carry a firearm outside the home is fundamental, the court applied strict scrutiny. It held that the Commonwealth failed to articulate a compelling interest supporting § 6108, so the statute violated the Equal Protection Clause of the Fourteenth Amendment as applied to this defendant. The court therefore vacated his judgment of sentence.
